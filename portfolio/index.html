Case 1

Running a YouTube Shorts channel for a personal-brand entrepreneur

Freelance, remote · Jul 2025 – May 2026 · The client stays anonymous.

Problem

The channel published clips of her podcast, but posting was irregular and most clips had very few views. I had about two hours a day to make each one count.

What I did

  • Chose clips with data (CTR, views and retention) instead of by feel.
  • Wrote a seven-step procedure for scheduling shorts, with time estimates, and logged every task in Toggl.
  • Sent one-page weekly reports and monthly summaries with proposals.
  • Tested three title options per video, based on three months of Analytics.
  • Defined five clip-selection criteria after studying the shorts that did best.

Result (rounded, always with its period)

+30%weekly views in one week of Jul 2025, after rescuing low-view, high-CTR clips (an early signal)
+41%views in Apr 2026 compared with Feb 2026
4.8%click-through rate in Apr 2026, the best of the quarter
~900views in under a day for one reworked short (Mar 2026)
~11.5kviews and ~280 watch hours in May 2026, about 70 above the usual

The target set for the channel, more than 1,000 views for most shorts, was not reached. I report it as it is, and it shaped how I work now.

What I would do next

  • A final check before anything is published: guest name, episode link, closing banner, music volume, resolution and subtitles.
  • A one-page agreement at the start: what each delivery includes, rounds of corrections, and what happens with rework.
  • A three-line confirmation after every task: what I understood, what I will do, what I need.

Skills it shows

  • Turning analytics into decisions and plain-language reports.
  • Documenting a process so it can be estimated and handed over.
  • Verifying AI output against the source before sending it.

Case 2

Head to Hand: a free tool, a landing page and a qualifying funnel

Final project of the 10-day Executive Assistant Bootcamp (Pareto Talent) · Oct 2026 · practice project, not an official Pareto page

Problem

Founders who run everything from memory cannot hand work off. The brief: design a free lead magnet and a funnel that moves the right founders toward a call.

What I built

  • A free app: six questions in about seven minutes turn one process into a one-page handoff brief, plus a list of what is still missing.
  • A landing page that starts from the founder's problem, cites its sources and says honestly who the offer is not for.
  • A qualifying form with three gates (role, revenue, timing) that sends qualified founders to a booking page and the rest to a thank-you page with the resource.
  • The back end: a pipeline and automated follow-ups in HighLevel, five ads, and a short video walkthrough.

The form redirects and the automations were tested on both paths (qualified and not qualified). The first email was blocked by the training account's shared sending limit, which was outside my setup. No real traffic: it was built for the bootcamp. Every number on the landing page comes from Pareto Talent's own website, and the page says so.
